01 · FLAGSHIP · BEGAN AS A COMMUNITY PROJECT
Live ↗

Avants AI

Avants began with the Community Edition—my first AI community project—before expanding into a broader recruiting platform for candidates, employers, and contributors.

Problem

Recruiters may give a résumé only an initial 7.4-second screen, leaving strong candidates vulnerable to being overlooked before their experience is understood. Length, unclear formatting, and avoidable typos can consume that narrow window, while a résumé alone still reveals little about how someone thinks or communicates. View the eye-tracking study ↗

Method

I mapped both sides of the hiring journey—discovery, applications, matching, résumé analysis, interviewing, evaluation, scheduling, and hiring—then built AI into the moments where additional context could improve a decision.

Solution

A recruiting platform with AI résumé scoring, job matching, voice and chat interviews, candidate coaching, employer review tools, meetings, messaging, and an Earn marketplace for paid AI-training tasks.

How it started

The Community Edition was the original project: a free, community-focused space for interview practice, résumé insight, career preparation, and paid AI-training opportunities. I later built the employer and recruiting side around that foundation.

Assistive 3D Prints for the Shepherd Center

Shepherd Center is an Atlanta rehabilitation hospital specializing in spinal-cord injury, brain injury, stroke, and other complex neurological care. As a volunteer leader, I used CAD and additive manufacturing to turn specific accessibility needs into practical tools for daily life.

Problem

Patients may need customized support for writing, holding personal items, grooming, dressing, showering, or attaching accessories to wheelchairs with different dimensions.

Method

I translated each use case into measurements and constraints, modeled the device in CAD, revised its fit and geometry, and prepared it for reliable 3D printing.

Solution

I produced 11 fabrication-ready designs: writing and squiggle splints, a U-cuff mount, dressing tool, adjustable mirror, cup and toothbrush holders, two deodorant holders, and two shower brackets.

Biomedical moonshot concept
PhotUP

Precision Lymphoma Therapy Concept

A research and design exploration of photodynamic nanocarriers for targeting Hodgkin lymphoma while reducing collateral damage to healthy immune cells.

Problem

Conventional photodynamic therapy has limited tissue penetration, while treatment can also harm healthy white blood cells and further weaken the immune system.

Method

Model an approach using upconversion nanoparticles to absorb near-infrared light and emit higher-frequency light near the target tissue.

Proposed solution

A localized UCNP-assisted photosensitizer concept intended to concentrate reactive-oxygen activity around affected lymphocytes and reduce light scatter into healthy cells.

how I got started

My Timeline

2023

Starting with robotics—and protecting the opportunity.

I created and led a robotics program at my school while working with Horizons concurrently. When the school moved to take the program away, I advocated for it to continue rather than letting students lose the opportunity. That experience—building a program, seeing how fragile access can be, and carrying the work into Horizons—became the foundation for my commitment to expanding STEM access.

NEXT

Learning AI through Chatnova and RHYMGEN.

I began with small experiments: Chatnova taught me how conversational AI products are structured, while the RHYMGEN image generator introduced me to model APIs, prompt-based generation, asynchronous JavaScript, validation, and the practical limits of AI systems. These were learning projects—the place where I first figured out how to turn an AI capability into a usable interface.

NEXT

Creating Avants AI Community Edition.

Once I understood the building blocks, I moved beyond standalone demos and created the Avants AI Community Edition—my first AI community project. It began with accessible résumé insight, interview practice, career preparation, and opportunities to contribute to AI-training work; the employer and recruiting platform grew from that foundation.

THEN

Building AI around the Minorities in STEM mission.

At the same time, Minorities in STEM became the community where new AI ideas could solve real access problems. We began kickstarting projects that help students learn AI, get personalized STEM support, find opportunities, organize resources, and build confidence using emerging technology.

2026

A growing ecosystem of community AI products.

AI Literacy Academy, STEMbud, Avants AI, the MIS platform, community hackathons, and related experiments now connect one technical journey: learn the tools, build something useful, listen to the community, and scale what works.

NOW

Using AI as infrastructure for opportunity.

I’m focused on building responsible products that help more people learn, demonstrate their abilities, access work, and participate in technology—not AI for its own sake.
