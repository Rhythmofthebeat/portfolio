The story

How it all spiraled.

From a couple of first builds to a movement — and back into building AI for the community.

Phase 01
The start

Image Generator & ChatNova

My first two builds — a generative image tool and an AI chat assistant, ChatNova. This is where I fell for building things from scratch.

Phase 02
First community project

Avants AI

Avants AI was my first project built for a community, not just for myself — the moment building became about people and impact.

Phase 03
CS spirals out

Machine learning, crypto & the web

From there, computer-science projects spiraled — machine learning, an ERC-20 smart contract, web platforms, and AI-education tools.

Phase 04
A movement

Minorities in STEM

It all converged into Minorities in STEM — turning personal building into access, community, and opportunity for thousands of students.

Phase 05
AI, for community

The AI projects spiral again

After Minorities in STEM, the AI community projects spiraled out again — STEMbud, AI Literacy, Who Gets to Build?, UpGive, and NITJAM — this time building AI tools squarely for people and impact.
