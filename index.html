AI Literacy Academy

ProblemStudents get access to AI tools long before they get access to AI education, so most use AI without understanding how it actually works.
SolutionA free 31-lesson curriculum across beginner, intermediate, and expert tracks, plus an AI encyclopedia. OpenAI calls run through a server-side Supabase edge function so no key ever reaches the browser.

The timeline

How it all spiraled.

From a chess program in 9th grade to AI platforms with real users, in the order it actually happened.

Phase 01
9th grade

Chess and machine learning

Where the coding started. I wrote a chess engine in Python with its own move search, then went straight into machine learning notebooks: logistic regression, neural networks, a CNN, and NLP on financial text. Nothing here was for anyone else yet. It was just learning how the machine thinks.

PythonpygameJupyterNeural networks
Phase 02
First real builds

RHYMGEN and ChatNova, my first AI projects

My first AI projects, and where I learned to code with AI. RHYMGEN turned prompts into images with Stable Diffusion, and ChatNova was a full-stack conversational assistant with accounts, saved history, and its own API. This is where I fell for shipping things from scratch instead of just running notebooks.

JavaScriptTypeScriptReactNode.jsMongoDBOpenAI API
Phase 03
Building for people

Avants AI, and Minorities in STEM

Avants AI was the turn. A free resume analyzer and matcher, it was the first thing I built for a community instead of for myself, and it now serves 1,000+ users through KR3. It ran alongside founding Minorities in STEM, and the two reframed everything: code as access, not as a hobby.

PythonFlaskNext.jsReact
Phase 04
The AI projects spiral

AI Literacy, STEMbud, Laptop Tracker, Cyber Defense

From there it kept multiplying. The AI Literacy Academy and STEMbud brought AI education to students directly, while at KR3 I shipped the Laptop Tracker, the Asset Tracker, and a cyber defense command platform as an intern department head. Same tools, much bigger stakes.

ReactViteExpressSupabaseOpenAI APIDocker
Phase 05
Now

Platforms at scale

The current work is infrastructure other people depend on: UpGive moving surplus corporate goods to nonprofits, the NITJAM portal serving 15,000+ alumni, Who Gets to Build?, and the i-Pledge Carbon Impact Platform with Microsoft's CELA team and IISD.
